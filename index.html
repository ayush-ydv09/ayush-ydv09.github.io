01 / About

Hardware-aware software. Practical engineering.

My interests sit where firmware meets electronics: microcontroller peripherals, sensor acquisition, communication interfaces, embedded control, and the logic that makes a system behave predictably. I’m building depth through hands-on projects and careful testing rather than collecting tools for their own sake.
