01 / ESP32 · SENSOR SYSTEMS · EMBEDDED C++● IN DEVELOPMENT

IoT-Based Food Spoilage Monitoring & Alerting System

A sensor-based prototype exploring whether environmental and gas-response signals can support food-condition monitoring. The ESP32 acquires temperature, humidity and gas-sensor readings, presents system data on an OLED, and provides a local alert output.

Engineering focus: sensor warm-up, baseline collection, humidity-related response, drift, repeatability and cross-sensitivity. Gas-sensor readings are treated as indicators to characterize—not as a standalone proof that food is spoiled.
